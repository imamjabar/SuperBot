# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Imam and the people he lets in talk to their bots (SuperBot): mostly on a phone between other things, sometimes on the Mac app at a desk. They ask for research, quick answers and long jobs, often in Indonesian and English in the same chat, and come back later to read results.

## Product Purpose

SuperBot is a self-hosted Grok Bot clone that is better on responsiveness, reliability, control and efficiency. One main chat per bot: every message gets an answer or an acknowledgement within seconds, long work runs as jobs that report back once, and the person stays in control of risky actions. Success: the chat feels as quick and calm as Grok on a cheap phone.

## Brand Personality

Quick, calm, capable. The interface stays out of the way: answers read like a person texting, not a dashboard. Confidence comes from speed and clear state, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Dashboard chrome in the chat: cards around every answer, badges, stat tiles.
- Oversized "accessible" phone type that shows half an answer per screen.
- Glassmorphism, gradients and motion that do not report state.
- Generic SaaS chat widgets.

## Design Principles

1. Grok Bot is the base for chat layout, density and flow; Aven adds clean visual polish only.
2. Light on low-end phones: no effect that costs frames or battery for looks.
3. The same type scale on every screen; phones get layout changes, not bigger text.
4. State over decoration: progress, waiting and failure are always visible and plain.
5. Familiar affordances: standard chat patterns before invented ones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Inputs stay at 16px on touch screens so iOS does not zoom. Right-to-left scripts get a taller line. Motion respects reduced-motion settings.
